Audit scope and source witnesses

The audit covers story prose across Episodes 1–8, including the Tea Parties. Support scripts, graphics, UI, and runtime behavior are excluded.

Original Witch Hunt is checked against the original-PC Japanese recovered with the historical patches. MangaGamer and Umineko Project are judged separately against the maintained PS3-derived Japanese. This is not a pristine retail PS3 extraction; the MangaGamer witness is the accepted backport described above.

Yellow annotations identify Material added from original PC script. They have no defect category and never contribute to the error totals.
