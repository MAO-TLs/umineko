MAO Umineko 1.0.0 — Source and testing notes

Text sources

The translation uses Japanese PC text and the maintained PS3-derived
Japanese script distributed by Umineko Project. The latter is an adapted
script, rather than a certified transcription of every retail PS3 asset.
Umineko Project also supplies the engine framework and presentation.

A few supplementary readings remain uncertain. The lyrics for “birth of
new witch,” “約束” and “霧のピトス” use Japanese transcriptions; some readings
differ between sources and have not been checked against official lyric
sheets. One Episode 2 results phrase and the Episode 6 result statuses
also rely on secondary Japanese transcriptions.

Voices

Both editions retain the PS3 performances. In MAO PC, some spoken lines
therefore differ from the Japanese PC text underlying the translation.
PC-only additions without corresponding recordings are unvoiced.

Testing

Both editions passed checks covering all 163 script chapters: manuscript
text, engine controls, voice calls, text colors, page flow and pack decoding.
Shared interface content, asset references and file integrity were checked.
Ending images and overlays were inspected directly. Song subtitle layout
was checked with offline rendering, and source cue timings were preserved.
The final ZIP was extracted and its installed files checked against hashes.

Selected screens and routes were checked in-game during development.
A complete playthrough and direct listening verification of every subtitle
have not been performed. These checks do not claim exhaustive coverage of
every scene, screen or configuration.
